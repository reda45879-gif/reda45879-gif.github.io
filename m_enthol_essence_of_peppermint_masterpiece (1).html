MOLECULAR PROFILE

(−)-Menthol

The dominant crystalline monoterpenoid alcohol in peppermint oil. Its (1R,2S,5R) chair conformation places all three bulky substituents in equatorial positions, selectively activating TRPM8 cold-sensitive ion channels.

  • Molecular Formula C₁₀H₂₀O
  • Molar Mass 156.27 g/mol
  • Typical Oil Fraction 38% – 48%
  • Boiling Point 212 °C
  • Primary Bioactivity TRPM8 Cooling & Analgesic

BOTANICAL SOURCE

Mentha × piperita L. (Lamiaceae)

A sterile natural hybrid of watermint (Mentha aquatica) and spearmint (Mentha spicata). Essential oil is synthesized and stored inside peltate glandular trichomes on the abaxial leaf surface.

  • Harvest Stage Full Bloom (Peak Menthol)
  • Oil Yield (Dry Leaf) 1.2% – 3.0% v/w
  • Refractive Index (20 °C) 1.459 – 1.465

TRPM8 Cold Analgesia

Activates calcium-permeable TRPM8 thermoreceptors, providing topical relief for tension headaches and myalgia.

GI Antispasmodic

Blocks L-type Ca²⁺ channels in intestinal smooth muscle, clinically validated in enteric-coated IBS capsules.

Antimicrobial Activity

Disrupts bacterial lipid bilayers and inhibits quorum-sensing biofilms in S. aureus and E. coli.

Respiratory Clearance

Synergy between menthol and 1,8-cineole enhances perceived nasal airflow and mucociliary clearance.
